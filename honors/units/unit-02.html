Unit 02 — Honors

Absolute Value Functions, Equations, and Inequalities

TEKS: A2.1.A–G (process, embedded throughout), A2.2.A, A2.6.C, A2.6.D, A2.6.E, A2.6.F

This unit takes a close look at the absolute value function: what its graph looks like and what makes it special, how stretching, compressing, reflecting, and shifting the graph changes it, and how to set up and solve equations and inequalities that involve absolute value. Honors pushes further with algebraic derivation, error-analysis, and extension problems throughout.
