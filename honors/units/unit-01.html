Unit 01 — Honors

Introduction to Functions in Algebra II

TEKS: A2.1.A–G (process, embedded throughout), A2.2.A, A2.2.B, A2.2.C, A2.2.D, A2.7.I

This unit covers three big ideas: describing a function's domain and range in different ways, exploring the seven "parent" functions and their key features, and understanding inverse functions — what they are, when they're also functions, and how to check that two functions really are inverses of each other. Honors pushes further with algebraic derivation, error-analysis, and extension problems throughout.
