Unit 03

Systems of Linear Equations and Inequalities

TEKS: A2.1.A–G (process, embedded throughout), A2.3.A, A2.3.B, A2.3.E, A2.3.F, A2.3.G

This unit is about solving several equations or inequalities at once. You'll start with systems of three linear equations in three variables — writing them from a situation, then solving them with a few different methods, including substitution, elimination, and matrices. Then you'll move to systems of linear inequalities: graphing them, shading in the region where every condition is true at the same time, and using that region to figure out the best or worst possible outcome in a real situation, an idea called linear programming.
